Terms, Community Rules & Privacy

Last updated: October 2026. Amoriva is operated by Ngeli Cyber, Kenya. Contact: support@example.com

What Amoriva is

A free community where adults practise languages and share their cultures by chatting with other members. Amoriva is supported by advertising. It is not a job, members are not paid to chat, and Amoriva never charges fees to join or use chat.

Who can join

You must be 18 or older. One account per person. Use your own identity — no impersonation or fake profiles.

Community rules

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech or threats.
  • No sexual content, and no romance or “companionship” services.
  • Never ask other members for money, gifts, airtime, M-Pesa or bank details, and never send them.
  • No spam, advertising, recruiting or links to outside schemes.
  • Don't share other people's personal information.

We may remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules. Use Block and Report in any chat to protect yourself.

Privacy

What we store: your email (for login), display name, country, languages, interests, bio, online status, and your messages. Data is stored with Google Firebase.

Who sees it: other signed-in members see your profile and online status. Messages are visible only to you and the person you're chatting with, and to moderators reviewing a report.

Ads: ads are provided by Google AdSense, which may use cookies to show and measure ads. See Google's ad policies for details.

Deleting your data: go to Profile → Delete account. This removes your profile, status, chat list and login. Messages you already sent remain visible to their recipients. You can also email us to request deletion.

No warranty

Amoriva is provided as-is. You are responsible for what you share with other members.
